SCADA Studio Tank station

Design a screen → bind its tags → deploy → operate and verify.

Start here · one screen, one experiment

Build (10–15 min): in Edit, add a lamp and bind it to P101. Select, drag and configure components. Deploy, then click the pump on the Run screen. Observe the tank and trend.

Compare (30–35 min): complete P1, P2 and I1 below this Studio. Load baseline keeps your layout but sets a repeatable 60 s input. Edit the requested setting, apply and redeploy for a controlled comparison. I2 is an extension to finish after class.

Extend to two tanks: in Screen practice, click Add TK102 station. Use the station selector for each tank’s scale, alarm and ACK target. Each tank has its own pump and level; scroll down inside the canvas at 100% or use Fit to see both. Adding a component copies a graphic; adding a station creates independent state. P3/I3 are extension or homework tasks.

Interpret: LT101 is the displayed level tag. Raw counts × scale = displayed metres. GOOD means recent data, not proven accuracy. HIGH activates after the on-delay and clears at HIGH − deadband. ACK records operator awareness; it does not repair the process.

Save (5 min): capture each completed test, explain the comparison, then download the report and evidence at the end. Allow additional time for questions.
